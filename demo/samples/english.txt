The morning rain had just stopped, and the lights came on in the little café on the corner. The owner opened the window and hung a small chalkboard by the door. It said: “Today’s first cup is free for our first guest.” Before long, a girl with a school bag ran in and asked, out of breath: “Excuse me, does hot chocolate count too?” The owner laughed, nodded, and turned to warm the milk. Outside, the sunlight slowly broke through the clouds and fell on the wet stone street. Holding the cup in both hands, the girl said quietly: “Thank you. This is the happiest moment of my whole week.” The owner thought for a moment and replied: “Then it’s a deal. Be here on time tomorrow, too.” Sometimes, one kind word is all it takes to make an ordinary day feel different.
