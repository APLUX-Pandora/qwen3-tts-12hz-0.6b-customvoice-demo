Native dependencies
----------------------
The SDK statically includes the OpenSSL crypto library (Apache-2.0).
See LICENSES/OpenSSL-copyright.txt and LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt for the original terms.
Model files use the AIDEM format and are loaded automatically by the matching SDK.
